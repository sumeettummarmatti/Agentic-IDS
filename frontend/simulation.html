Before you enter the Attack Lab

The Attack Lab is a safe sandbox for demos. While it is open:

  • Live threat analysis is paused. Traffic from the real and simulated servers is skipped, so you only see the attack you launch.
  • Skipped traffic is not analysed later. The dashboard history will have a gap.
  • Analysis resumes automatically when you go back to the dashboard or close this page.
